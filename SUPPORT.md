## Common questions

**What devices does FloorCraft work on?**
FloorCraft requires an iPhone or iPad with a LiDAR sensor — iPhone 12 Pro / 12 Pro Max or newer Pro models, and iPad Pro (2020) or newer. FloorCraft Pro runs on any Apple Silicon or Intel Mac with macOS 14 or later.

**Can I share scans between my iPhone and Mac?**
Yes. Sign in on both devices with the same account and your scans sync automatically. Edits made on one device appear on the other once both are online.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → FloorCraft → Cancel. Your subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period, then reverts to the free tier.

**I need to delete my account**
Open the app → Account → **Delete account**. This permanently removes your scans, teams, palettes and purchased pack credits from our servers. The action is irreversible — once completed there is no way to recover your data.

**My scan is missing a room / feature**
LiDAR scanning works best when you move slowly, cover every wall with a steady sweep, and keep the room well lit. For missing features (sinks, hobs, etc.), you can add them manually in the editor. For rooms the LiDAR couldn't detect (e.g. showers behind curtains), use the post-scan review sheet to add them.

**What happens to my LiDAR depth data?**
The raw LiDAR depth frames are processed entirely on your device by Apple's RoomPlan framework and never leave your phone. Only the simplified floor plan — rooms, walls, openings, fixtures — is saved and synced.
