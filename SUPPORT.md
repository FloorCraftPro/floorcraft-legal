## Common questions

**What devices does FloorCraft work on?**
FloorCraft requires an iPhone or iPad with a LiDAR sensor — iPhone 12 Pro / 12 Pro Max or newer Pro models, and iPad Pro (2020) or newer.

**Can I share scans between my iPhone and Mac?**
Yes. Sign in on both devices with the same Apple ID and your scans sync automatically.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → FloorCraft → Cancel.

**I need to delete my account**
Open the app → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your scans, teams, palettes and purchased credits from our servers.
